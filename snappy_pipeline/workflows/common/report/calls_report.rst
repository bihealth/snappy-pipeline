The calls of the VCF as a table, one row per call: with varlociraptor calls the posterior probability of every event and per sample the allele frequency with its posterior density, the depth, the genotype, the observations (reads for the alternative allele to the right, for the reference to the left, one bar per strength of evidence) and the biases found; with other callers the genotype, allele frequency, alt reads, depth and genotype quality of each sample.
